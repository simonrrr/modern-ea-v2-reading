# Slide vs source: what the reading-list research found (17 Nov 2026)

Fix the slide, or keep the caveat on the reading list. Not published (only `index.html` is).

## Wrong on the slide
- **Churchill (We shape our buildings):** House of **Commons**, 28 Oct 1943, not the House of Lords. Hansard: "afterwards".
- **DORA, Article 17:** the quote is from Article 17 ("ICT change management") of **Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1774** (the RTS under DORA). DORA's own Article 17 is incident management. Suggested credit: "DORA RTS (EU) 2024/1774, Art. 17, ICT change management".
- **Velitchkov, Essential Balances:** published **2020** (Kvistgaard), not 2026 (slides 36 and 62).
- **Allan Kelly:** a **2014** talk (London Continuous Delivery meetup); 2018 is the transcript notes' date.
- **Hohpe clip (slide 41):** "YOW!, 2026" — the talk is **YOW! 2017** (repeated 2018). Check which recording the clip is from.
- **Cynefin triquetra:** "© Cognitive Edge Ltd" — the triquetra (May 2026) is **The Cynefin Company**'s.
- **Brooks (slide 46 image):** title is "No Silver Bullet: Essence and **Accidents** of Software Engineering".

## Attribution to check
- **Eddleston, "conditions may be tended…":** found in his Substack post (6 May 2026), not verified in the book the slide credits.
- **Miell, "…financial structures and incentives…":** this wording is Jamie Dobson's statement of "Miell's Law" (Substack, 5 Jun 2026), not found in the (early-release) book.
- **Conway, slide 45 "…will produce a design whose structure is a copy…":** Conway's later restatement on melconway.com, not in the 1968 paper. Slide 21's line is from the paper.
- **Gene Kim:** source is BMK's live-tweet of his DevOps masterclass, **14 July 2022** (slide says 2023).
- **Korzybski:** he wrote "A map is not the territory it represents"; first printed 1933 (paper given 1931).
- **Hohpe:** a comment on his LinkedIn post of 8 Mar 2026 (comments need sign-in).
- **Fujio Cho:** first-hand source is John Shook (LEI, 2011), not a Cho text.
- **IBM 1979:** provenance via Simon Willison and Mike Hoye (Mastodon, 2024: the "Computer Mandate" pages seen through the photo). The Reddit link in the AMAA deck could not be recovered from the file.
- **Wardley doctrine, "Steve Purkis variation":** no published Purkis table found.
